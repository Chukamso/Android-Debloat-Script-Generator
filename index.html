🧹 A06 Debloat Interface

Select packages to remove bloatware from your device

📋 How to Use:

  1. Select the packages you want to remove by clicking on them
  2. Click "Generate ADB Script" to create the removal commands
  3. Enable USB Debugging on your Android device
  4. Connect your device to PC and run: adb devices
  5. Copy and paste the generated commands into your terminal
  6. Note: Some packages may require root access
